Quiznova Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

1. Who we are

Quiznova ("the app", "we", "us") is a free Android quiz and rewards-points app published by Harsh Verma, an individual developer in India.

Contact: therajpoot2007@gmail.com

2. What the app does

You can play short quizzes, spin a wheel, claim a daily bonus and watch video ads to collect in-app coins. Coins are points that are kept inside the app. They are not money, and we do not promise any earnings.

3. Information we collect

Account information. When you sign in, we receive your sign-in details through Firebase Authentication:

  • With Google sign-in: your name, email address and a unique account ID.
  • With email and password: your email address and a unique account ID. Your password is handled by Firebase and is not visible to us.

Game data. We store your coin balance, daily streak, the time of your last bonus, and how many quiz rounds and spins you have used today. This is stored in Cloud Firestore under your account ID.

Ad and device data. To show ads, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can reveal approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This is collected by Google, not by us directly.

We do not ask for your phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.

4. How we use information

  • To sign you in and keep your coins and progress saved.
  • To enforce daily limits and cooldowns and to keep the app fair.
  • To show ads, which help us keep the app free.
  • To reply to your questions and requests.

5. Ads

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. These include video ads that you can choose to watch for coins, and a short ad after some quiz rounds and spins. You never need to click an ad to get coins.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads). Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

6. Third-party services

The app uses these services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Sign-In and Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore): firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): policies.google.com/privacy

7. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers listed above, who process it to run the app, and when the law requires us to.

8. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. To delete your account and the game data linked to it, email us at therajpoot2007@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign in. We will delete it within 30 days. Data held by Google for ads is governed by Google's own policies.

9. Security

Your data is stored with Google Firebase, and our database rules allow each signed-in user to access only their own game data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

The app is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has used the app, email us and we will delete that account.

11. No affiliation

Quiznova is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any game company. Any game names that appear in quiz questions belong to their respective owners.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. If we make important changes, we will tell you in the app.
